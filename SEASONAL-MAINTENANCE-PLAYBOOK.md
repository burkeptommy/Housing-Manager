# Seasonal Maintenance Playbook — The Property Manager's Default

What a professional property manager does for a $2-5M home over a full year. Focus is value preservation and failure prevention, not cosmetic maintenance. Budget: 1-2% of home value annually, which for a $3M home is $30-60K in vendor spend + materials.

Every task is tagged:
- **Who:** DIY (homeowner, ≤60 min) / Handyman (bundled visits) / Specialist (licensed trade)
- **Cost:** typical range
- **Why it matters:** value preservation rationale

Cadences:
- **Monthly** — filter / inspection rhythm
- **Quarterly** — accumulating small-vendor work
- **Seasonal** — the four big coordination moments
- **Annual** — once per year, dated to a season
- **Multi-year** — every 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 years

---

## Summer (Jun–Aug) — Cooling, irrigation, exterior stress

Summer is the stress-test season. AC runs constantly, irrigation runs constantly, UV + heat degrade exterior finishes, storms drive gutters + drainage checks. Property managers front-load the summer work to June so systems aren't failing in 95°F heat.

### HVAC — Specialist
- **Cooling tune-up** — Annual, spring/early summer. $150-350. Technician cleans condenser coils, checks refrigerant charge, tests capacitor, flushes condensate line. **Why:** a dirty condenser coil drops efficiency 10-20% and shortens compressor life by years — compressors are the $3-6K failure.
- **Replace air filters** — Monthly DIY (every 30-60 days in cooling season). $15-40/filter. **Why:** restricted airflow = frozen evaporator coil = burned-out blower motor.
- **Inspect duct leaks** — Every 2-3 years, $200-400. **Why:** 20-30% of conditioned air is often lost through duct leaks.
- **Clean dryer vent** — Annual, $100-200. **Why:** #1 cause of home fires per NFPA. Do in summer when dryer runs less so downtime doesn't matter.

### Landscaping — Specialist (weekly) + Handyman (seasonal)
- **Weekly mowing + edging** — $75-200/visit in season. **Why:** weekly cadence keeps grass at 3-4" which shades soil, conserves water, outcompetes weeds.
- **Irrigation startup + audit** — Spring/early summer, $200-400. Tech checks head coverage, adjusts timers, repairs broken heads. **Why:** a single stuck-open zone wastes 10K+ gallons/month; brown patches drop curb appeal.
- **Mulch beds** — Annual spring, $400-1,200. **Why:** 3" of mulch reduces water use 25% and prevents weed germination.
- **Summer fertilization + grub treatment** — Once in June, $150-400. **Why:** unchecked grub damage shows up as brown lawn patches in August that cost $2-5K to reseed.
- **Mosquito & tick spray** — Every 3 weeks, Apr-Oct, $80-120/treatment. **Why:** unmanaged outdoor spaces reduce effective square footage + Lyme exposure.
- **Tree inspection** — Annual, $150-400. Arborist flags dead limbs + structural weakness before storm season. **Why:** one dropped oak limb = $5-50K in roof / car / deck / fence damage.

### Pool / Spa — Specialist (weekly)
- **Pool opening** — Annual May, $300-600. Removes cover, balances chemistry, starts pump, inspects equipment. **Why:** skipping opening protocols damages surfaces + pump seals.
- **Weekly chemistry + cleaning** — $150-250/visit. **Why:** imbalanced water pits plaster / vinyl (both $10-30K to redo), damages heater copper, degrades equipment seals.
- **Pump + filter inspection** — Annual mid-summer, $100-250. **Why:** a failing pump during peak use = green pool in 3 days.
- **Safety fence / cover inspection** — Annual, $0-200. **Why:** insurance + liability; cracked latches fail silently.

### Exterior & Hardscape — Handyman / Specialist
- **Pressure wash siding + deck** — Annual late spring, $300-700. **Why:** mildew + algae degrade paint and wood 3-5x faster than sun alone.
- **Deck staining / sealing** — Every 2-3 years, $500-1,500. **Why:** deferred sealing = board replacement at $30-60/sq ft vs $3-5/sq ft for re-stain.
- **Gutter cleaning (summer)** — After spring pollen drop + any tree-debris event, $150-300. **Why:** clogged gutters dump water at foundation = $10-40K foundation repair.
- **Window cleaning (interior + exterior)** — Twice yearly (spring + fall), $200-500. **Why:** UV through dirty glass damages interior fabrics; water spots etch glass permanently.
- **Inspect weatherstripping + caulking** — Annual summer, DIY or handyman. **Why:** dried-out seals leak conditioned air in summer AND heat in winter; pre-winter discovery costs less to fix.
- **Driveway sealcoating (asphalt)** — Every 3 years, $300-800. **Why:** sealing extends life from 15 to 25+ years and prevents the $5-15K repave.
- **Patio / paver joint sand top-up** — Every 2 years, $200-500. **Why:** empty joints = weed germination + paver shift + drainage failure.

### Electrical / Safety — Specialist
- **Generator load-test + oil change** — Quarterly (automatic transfer switches) or biannual. $150-350/visit. **Why:** a generator that fails during a storm is worse than no generator — drained batteries, flooded basements.
- **Smoke + CO detectors** — Test monthly, replace batteries twice yearly, replace units every 10 years. DIY. **Why:** insurance + life safety.
- **EV charger inspection** — Annual, $100-200. **Why:** arcing at the plug causes fires; cable wear is silent.
- **Electrical panel IR scan** — Every 2-3 years, $200-500. **Why:** hot spots = pre-failure signals; catches loose connections before they arc.

### Plumbing — Specialist (reactive) + DIY (monthly)
- **Test sump pump** — Quarterly DIY. **Why:** a failed sump during a summer thunderstorm = flooded finished basement, $20-80K.
- **Water heater flush** — Annual, $100-250. **Why:** sediment cuts tank life 30-50% and drops efficiency 10-15%.
- **Leak detection system test** — Annual if installed, $0-150. **Why:** insurance discounts; catches slow leaks at $50 instead of $20K.
- **Clean water softener / refill salt** — Monthly DIY, refills ~$15/month. **Why:** hard water destroys fixtures + appliances silently.

### Interior / Appliances — DIY + Specialist
- **Refrigerator coil cleaning** — Annual, DIY or $100-150. **Why:** adds 3-5 years of compressor life.
- **Range hood filter clean + duct check** — Annual, DIY. **Why:** grease fire prevention.
- **Inspect attic for heat / moisture** — Annual summer, DIY. **Why:** summer is when overheated attic damage shows up as buckled shingles + compromised insulation.

## Fall (Sep–Nov) — Winterization, heating, envelope sealing

Fall is the highest-leverage season for value preservation. Everything you don't do in October becomes an expensive emergency in January. Property managers book fall vendors in August because calendars fill.

### HVAC — Specialist
- **Heating tune-up / boiler service** — Annual, $200-450. Tech cleans burner assembly, checks heat exchanger for cracks, tests safety controls, bleeds radiators. **Why:** a cracked heat exchanger = carbon monoxide; catching it before ignition season saves the entire system.
- **Humidifier inspection + pad replacement** — Annual, $50-150. **Why:** dry winter air cracks millwork, hardwood, and art — humidifier failure costs tens of thousands in refinishing.
- **Chimney sweep + inspection** — Annual before first fire, $300-600. **Why:** creosote buildup is the #1 cause of chimney fires; a liner crack leaks CO.

### Landscaping — Specialist
- **Leaf cleanup (multi-pass)** — Oct-Dec, $300-800/season. **Why:** wet leaves left on lawn = dead grass + slip hazard + gutter clogs.
- **Fall aeration + overseeding** — Annual, $300-600. **Why:** fall is the only time to repair summer damage with full growth potential. Skipping = thin lawn + 5-10% curb appeal hit.
- **Winterize irrigation (blow-out)** — Annual before first freeze, $100-200. **Why:** skipping = cracked backflow preventer + underground pipe repair at $2-10K.
- **Tree pruning (structural)** — Late fall, $400-2,500. **Why:** pruning before snow load prevents storm damage; dormant pruning cuts heal cleaner.
- **Lawn + bed winterizer fertilization** — Annual, $100-300. **Why:** pre-winter root development = stronger spring green-up.

### Roof + Drainage — Specialist
- **Roof inspection** — Annual, $200-500. **Why:** 5 minutes of missing flashing = 5 feet of water damage inside walls.
- **Gutter cleaning (fall)** — Twice, before and after leaf drop, $200-400/visit. **Why:** ice dams form on clogged gutters — each ice dam event can dump $5-30K of water into the house.
- **Downspout extensions check** — Annual DIY. **Why:** water at foundation = basement leaks + frost heave cracks.
- **Heat cable inspection (if installed)** — Annual, $100-200. **Why:** heat cables that fail during ice storms don't get emergency-fixed in January.

### Plumbing / Water — Specialist
- **Exterior faucet (hose bib) shutoff + drain** — Annual DIY or handyman. **Why:** frozen hose bib = split pipe inside wall = $5-30K water damage.
- **Water heater anode rod check** — Every 3 years, $150-300. **Why:** anode replacement at $250 vs. tank replacement at $2,500.
- **Sump pump + battery backup test** — Fall DIY. **Why:** thaw-freeze cycles in March drive sump runtime — failures surface when basement is already wet.
- **Septic pump-out (scheduled years)** — Every 3-5 years, $400-800. **Why:** septic failure mid-winter = frozen ground + $15-50K drainfield replacement.
- **Well system inspection + water test** — Annual, $200-500. **Why:** winter is when a failing pump fails — annual check catches dropping pressure early.

### Envelope / Safety — Handyman + Specialist
- **Weatherstripping inspection** — Annual fall, handyman bundle. **Why:** leaky doors + windows = 15-25% higher heating bill + cold spots that drive thermostat wars.
- **Caulk exterior penetrations** — Annual fall, handyman. **Why:** pest + water entry points; $20 of caulk prevents $5K rodent remediation.
- **Attic insulation + ventilation check** — Every 5 years, $200-400 inspection. **Why:** under-insulated attics drive ice dams + $500+/month heating bills.
- **Smoke + CO detectors** — Fall battery swap + test. **Why:** heating season = highest CO risk.
- **Garage door balance + safety check** — Annual, $100-200. **Why:** spring failures during winter = stuck cars and broken openers.

### Outdoor Living — Handyman
- **Deck + patio furniture storage** — Annual DIY or $150-400 handyman. **Why:** UV-faded + water-damaged furniture = $5-15K replacement vs free storage.
- **Grill winterization / cover** — Annual DIY. **Why:** rust + mice in grill lines; gas grills fail when stored wet.
- **Pool closing** — Annual Oct-Nov, $400-800. **Why:** improper closing = cracked plumbing + liner damage at $5-25K.
- **Hot tub winterization (if not in use)** — Annual if seasonal, $150-400. **Why:** frozen jets crack the shell — full replacement cost.
- **Snow-plow contract signing** — Annual before Nov 1, $400-1,500/season. **Why:** last-minute contracts cost 2x and often don't cover priority plowing.

### Chimney / Fireplace — Specialist
- **Chimney sweep** — Annual, $200-400. **Why:** creosote fires + structural degradation.
- **Fireplace damper + gasket check** — Annual. **Why:** leaky dampers lose 8-10% of heated air.
- **Wood-stove gasket replacement** — Every 2-3 years, $100-200. **Why:** worn gaskets = CO leaks.

## Winter (Dec–Feb) — Protection, monitoring, emergency readiness

Winter is the watching-and-waiting season. Property managers do very little proactive work mid-winter — they monitor + respond. The key is that fall prep was done right.

### Daily / Weekly — DIY or Property Manager
- **Check basement + crawl space for moisture** — Weekly DIY after storms. **Why:** catches slow leaks before mold.
- **Monitor humidity** — Weekly DIY, aim 30-45%. **Why:** low humidity cracks millwork; high humidity condenses on windows + rots sills.
- **Clear snow from exhaust vents** — After every 4"+ snowfall. **Why:** blocked high-efficiency furnace vents + dryer vents cause CO poisoning.

### Monthly — DIY
- **Replace HVAC filter** — Monthly in heating season.
- **Test GFCI outlets** — Monthly.
- **Test smoke / CO detectors** — Monthly.
- **Flush one floor drain** — Monthly pour water to maintain trap seals.

### Event-Driven — Handyman + Specialist
- **Ice dam prevention** — Steam-remove if spotted, $400-1,500/visit. **Why:** ice dams in progress cause $10-50K damage if left even 48 hours.
- **Snow removal from roof overhang (if heavy)** — $200-600. **Why:** collapse risk on low-slope + porches.
- **Plumbing freeze response** — Heat-trace + open fixtures at < 15°F. **Why:** one burst pipe in a ski-house guest-wing = $30-100K repair + mold remediation.

### Quarterly — Specialist
- **Generator run-test** — Monthly auto, quarterly manual load-test. **Why:** generators that don't start in a power outage = frozen pipes + food loss + medical risk.
- **Water leak detector battery + test** — Quarterly. **Why:** dead sensors fail silently.

## Spring (Mar–May) — Damage assessment, system activation, prep

Spring is the audit season. Everything that took hits over winter gets logged and fixed before summer. Spring is the highest concentration of vendor coordination — property managers start booking in February.

### Roof / Envelope — Specialist
- **Roof + flashing inspection** — Annual post-winter, $200-500. **Why:** ice + wind damage shows up now; fixing a missing shingle is $200, fixing a rotten roof deck is $20K.
- **Gutter + downspout inspection** — Annual, $150-300. Check for ice-damage separations.
- **Siding + trim inspection** — Annual DIY walk + spot handyman repair. **Why:** loose trim = water entry in summer storms.
- **Exterior paint touch-up** — Annual spring, handyman. **Why:** full exterior repaint is $10-40K every 7-10 years vs $500/year in touch-ups keeping paint at 15+ years.

### HVAC — Specialist
- **Cooling tune-up** — See Summer.
- **Ductwork cleaning** — Every 3-5 years, $400-1,000. **Why:** accumulated dust reduces airflow + aggravates allergies.
- **Humidifier shutdown** — Annual DIY or handyman.

### Plumbing / Water — Specialist + DIY
- **Exterior faucet turn-on + leak check** — Annual DIY after last freeze. **Why:** winter-split pipes leak inside walls before you notice outside.
- **Sump pump test + battery backup** — Annual spring DIY.
- **Septic inspection** — Annual, $100-250 inspection. **Why:** winter ground movement can stress the system; catching early = less disruption.
- **Water softener / filtration service** — Annual, $100-300.
- **Irrigation startup** — See Summer.

### Landscaping — Specialist
- **Spring cleanup** — Annual Mar-Apr, $400-1,500. Debris removal, bed edging, first mow. **Why:** sets the tone for the entire growing season.
- **Pre-emergent herbicide** — Annual Apr, $100-300. **Why:** kills crabgrass seed before it germinates; reactive treatment mid-summer is 3x cost + lawn damage.
- **Tree fertilization (mature trees)** — Every 2-3 years, $150-400/tree. **Why:** a single mature oak is $10-50K to remove if it dies; fertilization extends life decades.
- **Mulch refresh** — See Summer.

### Pool / Spa — See Summer

### Safety / Inspection — Specialist
- **Radon test (where applicable)** — Every 2 years, $150-300. **Why:** radon increases lung cancer risk + hurts resale in disclosure states.
- **Pest inspection (termite + general)** — Annual, $100-300. **Why:** termite damage averages $8K when caught early, $30K+ when late.
- **Fire extinguisher check** — Annual DIY. **Why:** pressure loss renders them useless.

## Quarterly / Every Few Years (non-seasonal)

These don't map to a single season but property managers keep them on the calendar:

### Quarterly
- **Pest control treatment** — $80-150/visit. **Why:** quarterly barrier treatments keep carpenter ants, mice, wasps out.
- **Air filter swap (HVAC)** — DIY monthly to quarterly.
- **Test safety systems** — Smoke, CO, GFCI, sump pump.
- **Deep-clean one room/system** — Range hood, disposal, dishwasher filters, washer screens.

### Every 2-3 years
- **Duct cleaning** — $400-1,000. Triggered by renovation + every ~3 years.
- **Exterior paint touch-up full pass** — $500-2,000.
- **Deck staining** — $500-1,500.
- **Electrical panel IR scan** — $200-500.

### Every 5 years
- **Water heater anode rod check** — $150-300.
- **Sump pump replacement** — $500-1,500 (5-10 yr lifespan).
- **Driveway sealcoating** — $300-800.
- **Attic insulation top-up** — $500-2,500.
- **Exterior caulk + weatherstripping full pass** — $800-2,000.

### Every 7-10 years
- **Exterior paint (full)** — $8,000-30,000.
- **Roof inspection + minor repair reserve** — $500-2,000 (avoids $25-60K premature replacement).
- **HVAC replacement planning** — systems last 15-20 yr with maintenance, 10-12 without.
- **Water heater replacement** — $2,000-4,500.

## The Property Manager's Top-10 Value-Preservation Tasks (HNW ranked by ROI)

If a homeowner does nothing else, these prevent the highest-dollar failures:

1. **Annual HVAC tune-ups (both heating + cooling)** — $300-500/yr prevents $5-15K compressor/heat-exchanger failures
2. **Gutter cleaning 2x/year** — $300-600/yr prevents $10-40K foundation + ice-dam damage
3. **Annual roof inspection** — $200-500/yr prevents $20-60K premature replacement
4. **Annual chimney sweep (if fireplace used)** — $300-600/yr prevents house fires + CO
5. **Irrigation winterization + spring startup** — $300-600/yr prevents $2-10K pipe + backflow repair
6. **Weekly landscaping during growing season** — $3-10K/yr protects $50-200K+ of curb appeal
7. **Annual tree arborist inspection** — $150-400/yr prevents $5-50K storm damage claims
8. **Septic pump every 3-5 years** — $400-800 prevents $15-50K drainfield replacement
9. **Water heater flush annually** — $100-250/yr adds 5-10 years to tank life
10. **Annual generator service (if equipped)** — $200-400/yr difference between "power restored" and "frozen pipes + flooded basement"

## What property managers DON'T do for tenants (but HNW homeowners should)

These deliver value but are usually cut from rental property budgets:

- Paint touch-ups every year (rentals: every 5-7 years at turnover)
- Mature tree fertilization (rentals: let them die)
- Deck + fence staining on schedule (rentals: replace when falling over)
- Leak detection + smart water shutoff systems (rentals: no)
- Mosquito + tick barrier treatments (rentals: no)
- Annual radon / air-quality testing (rentals: only if required)
- Window cleaning interior + exterior 2x/year (rentals: never)
- IR electrical panel scans (rentals: only reactive)
- Attic insulation top-ups (rentals: only if tenant complains about bills)
