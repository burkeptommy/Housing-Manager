Chez SMS Coordination Program

Chez offers an optional SMS coordination feature that, where available, enables two-way text message communication between you and your home service vendors through a Chez-provided phone number.

Program name: Chez SMS Coordination

Description: When enabled, the SMS coordination feature allows you (the homeowner) to add vendor phone numbers to your account. Vendors can text the Chez number to confirm completed visits, indicate arrival, or send scheduling updates. Messages are relayed between vendors, the Chez system, and you. The system sends operational acknowledgments and clarification requests as needed.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on the number of vendors you have enabled and the frequency of their service visits. Typically 1 to 10 messages per week per vendor.
