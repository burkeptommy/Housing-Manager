Haven SMS Coordination Program

Haven offers an optional SMS coordination feature that enables two-way text message communication between you and your home service vendors through a Haven-provided phone number.

Program name: Haven SMS Coordination

Description: When enabled, the SMS coordination feature allows you (the homeowner) to add vendor phone numbers to your account. Vendors can text the Haven number to confirm completed visits, indicate arrival, or send scheduling updates. Messages are relayed between vendors, the Haven system, and you. The system sends operational acknowledgments and clarification requests as needed.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on the number of vendors you have enabled and the frequency of their service visits. Typically 1 to 10 messages per week per vendor.

Message and data rates: Message and data rates may apply.

Support: For help with the SMS program, email support@havenhome.dev or text HELP to the Haven number.

To opt out: Text STOP to the Haven number at any time to unsubscribe. Text START to resubscribe.

For help: Text HELP to the Haven number for information about the program.

By enabling SMS coordination, you authorize Haven to send and receive SMS messages on your behalf to the vendors you have added. You confirm that vendors you add have agreed to receive these messages.
