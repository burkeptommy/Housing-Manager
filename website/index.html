Snap → know

Point your camera at anything in your home. We'll know what it is.

Most home apps make you type model numbers. Chez uses Claude Vision to read the label off any appliance, HVAC plate, or VIN — then matches against a catalog of 219 brands and 2,818 models, surfacing manuals, recommended filters, and replacement parts.

  • Photo equipment ID. Make, model, serial, and product type — extracted in seconds. Filters and replacement parts surface automatically.
  • VIN photo capture. Snap the dashboard VIN. Decoded via NHTSA. Recalls scanned. AI-generated maintenance schedule attached to the vehicle.
  • Email forwarding pipeline. Forward bills, invoices, quotes, vendor docs to your private alfred address. AI categorizes into 88 document types and dedups by content hash.

Plan & schedule

Maintenance, organized by what's on schedule and what needs a decision.

The Maintenance Hub separates what's already happening (your services, your handyman, your vehicles) from what needs your attention (this season, upcoming visits). Two yearly handyman visits keep your home's value high — automatically bundled from your accumulated punch list.

  • House Quiz. 37 questions across 6 sections. Save and resume. Ends with a cinematic reveal of the dollar value your maintenance plan protects over 10 years.
  • Maintenance Hub. Five sections: Your Services, Next Handyman Visit, Vehicles, This Season, Upcoming Scheduled.
  • Routine management. Recurring services (cleaning, lawn, snow, pool) as first-class objects. Active months. Pause windows. One vendor per routine.
  • Two yearly handyman visits. Spring and fall punch-list bundles auto-built from small DIY items you've accumulated all year.
  • Important events. Family calendar, school days, birthdays, recurring deliveries, trash days — with morning-of and evening-before reminders.

Track & protect

Vehicles, documents, every important date — captured, encrypted, searchable.

Snap a photo of any VIN — get the full vehicle profile, NHTSA recall scan, and AI-generated maintenance schedule. Everything you forward to Chez is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and auto-categorized into 88 document types. Year at a Glance shows every vendor, every vehicle service, every family event on one timeline.

  • Vehicle management. VIN scan or manual entry. Covered drivers. Mechanic on file. NHTSA recall scanning. Registration and insurance expiry alerts.
  • Document Vault. AES-256-GCM encrypted. 88 categories. Full-text search. Party detection. Duplicate detection by content hash.

  • Activity Log. 14 event types, verb-first ("Invoice processed: Tyler Heating, $450"). Grouped by day. Always recoverable.
  • Push notifications. Tasks due, service visits incoming, recalls detected, document expirations 30 days out.

Why Chez

Premium design for premium homes.

Most home apps were built for renters or DIYers. Chez is built for households that have professionals doing the work — and need a quiet, intelligent layer between the homeowner, the family, and everyone they delegate to.

AI that creates intelligence

Forward a service invoice and Chez extracts line items, suggests follow-ups, and detects recurring cadence. Snap an appliance and it identifies the model, surfaces filters, and remembers it forever.

Vehicle intelligence, not just a list

Snap a VIN; Chez decodes the make/model/trim, pulls every NHTSA recall, builds an AI maintenance schedule, and links every invoice you forward to the right car automatically.

Designed for delegation

The Home Manager role lets your property manager work in Chez without ever seeing your financials or medical documents. Permissions enforced server-side. Trust by design.

One platform, both sides

Your handyman runs their business in Chez too. When they finish a visit at your home, the work, the photos, and the new system details flow back to your app — automatically.

Trust by design

Architecturally inaccessible to anyone but you.

Most cloud storage encrypts your files but anyone at the company with admin access can still see them. Chez adds row-level isolation and role-scoped document access enforced at the database layer.
