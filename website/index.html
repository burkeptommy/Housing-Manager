Why Chez exists

Every home app gets it wrong.

Hundreds of apps promise to help you run your home. They all do the same thing: tell you what needs doing, then leave the doing to you. Some go further in the wrong direction.

They remind. You do the work.

Task managers and checklists hand you a to-do list and walk away. The calls, the quotes, and the scheduling are still yours.

Some sell your data.

Your home, your systems, and your spending get packaged up and sold to whoever pays for it. Never here.

Some fake their growth.

Affiliate kickbacks and pay-to-play "recommendations" dressed up as referrals. We take none of it.

All of them stop at the list.

Even the best of them just organize the work. Not one of them picks up the phone and gets it done.

Chez is built by homeowners, for homeowners. A real person on our team does the actual work. We never sell your data. We take no affiliate kickbacks. Trust is the product.
