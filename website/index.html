Two sides of one platform

Built for the people who own the home, and the people who keep it running.

Homeowners use Chez as their iPhone command center. Handymen use Chez to dispatch crews, build quotes, run field visits, and grow their business. Same platform, two products designed for each other.

For Homeowners

A quiet command center for your home, your family, and your future.

Snap a photo of any appliance — we know the make, model, and replacement filters. Forward an invoice — we update your maintenance plan. Add a vendor — we know who covers what. Set up your estate once and hand it to your attorney with a click.

For homeowners For Handymen

Dispatch, quoting, scheduling, field execution, and CRM. One workspace.

Run your handyman business from a single place. Build quotes from reusable templates. Dispatch routes by technician with time windows. Run offline-capable field visits with checklists, photo capture, and homeowner notes. Get pre-organized leads from Chez homeowners.

For handymen
What Chez does

Intelligence that turns scattered details into a clear picture of your home.

Snap → know

Photograph any appliance label, HVAC plate, or VIN. We extract the make, model, serial, and matched parts in seconds.

Plan & schedule

Maintenance Hub organizes everything by what's on schedule and what needs a decision. Two yearly handyman visits to keep your home's value high.

Coordinate

Vendor management, routine management, family members, and a Home Manager role for your property staff — they manage tasks, never see the will.

Decide & invest

Project planning, quote comparison vs. fair market, active spend tracking, and ROI vs. equity gain. Alfred AI chat that knows your house.

Track & protect

Vehicles with NHTSA recall scanning, important family events, and an encrypted document vault that auto-categorizes everything you forward.

Estate & legacy

Estate intake captures fiduciaries, advisors, wishes, and concerns. Generate an attorney-ready PDF with a verifiable handoff link.

Why Chez

Premium design for premium homes.

Most home apps were built for renters or DIYers. Chez is built for households that have professionals doing the work — and need a quiet, intelligent layer between the homeowner, the family, and everyone they delegate to.

AI that creates intelligence

Forward a service invoice and Chez extracts line items, suggests follow-ups, and detects recurring cadence. Snap an appliance and it identifies the model, surfaces filters, and remembers it forever.

Estate intelligence, not just storage

Other apps store your will. Chez models it: fiduciaries linked to family members, readiness scoring, staleness detection, and a PDF handoff your attorney can verify in one click.

Designed for delegation

The Home Manager role lets your property manager work in Chez without ever seeing your estate, financials, or medical documents. Permissions enforced server-side. Trust by design.

One platform, both sides

Your handyman runs their business in Chez too. When they finish a visit at your home, the work, the photos, and the new system details flow back to your app — automatically.
