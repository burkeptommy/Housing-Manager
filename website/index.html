Your home runs on a spreadsheet at best. More likely, out of your junk drawer.

Insurance in one folder. Maintenance history in your head. Your will somewhere in a drawer. When something goes wrong, you scramble. Haven puts it all in one secure place so you never have to.

Everything in one app

Two pillars of a well-managed home

Haven covers the things that keep homeowners up at night: the house itself, the paperwork, and the planning.

Home Management

Track every system in your home and never miss maintenance again.

  • Property and home system tracking
  • Automated maintenance schedules
  • Vendor and contractor directory
  • Warranty tracking with expiration alerts
  • Service history and cost tracking

Estate & Life Documents

Organize the documents that protect your family and your assets.

  • AI reads and categorizes uploads instantly
  • Estate readiness score and gap analysis
  • Expiration tracking and renewal reminders
  • Family member document tagging
  • Missing document detection

How Haven Works

1

Add your home

Enter your property details and Haven builds your home profile with systems, maintenance schedules, and tracking automatically.

2

Upload your documents

Snap a photo or upload a PDF. Alfred reads it, pulls out key dates, and files it in the right category.

3

Let Alfred guide you

Alfred tells you what's missing, what's expiring, and what to do next. Ask it anything about your home or estate plan.

Built for families

Features that actually matter

Estate Readiness Score

See exactly how prepared your family is. Haven scores your coverage across wills, insurance, financial accounts, and more, and tells you what's missing.

Scenario Planning

Ask "what if we sold our house?" or "what happens if one of us dies?" Haven runs the analysis against your actual documents and data.

Family Sharing

Invite your spouse or partner to share the same household. Both of you see the same documents, properties, and maintenance from your own secure logins.

Smart Reminders

Get notified before documents expire, maintenance comes due, or warranties lapse. Haven watches the dates so you don't have to.

AI Document Analysis

Upload a document and Alfred reads it instantly. It extracts dates, identifies the type, summarizes the contents, and flags what needs attention.

Vendor Directory

Keep your plumber, electrician, HVAC tech, and every contractor in one place with contact info, specialties, and service history.
