The field app

An offline-capable mobile field app — free for every technician on your team.

Your technician opens the visit on their phone. The whole visit — checklist, photo capture, system identification, homeowner notes, recommendations, signoff — runs entirely on the device. No cell signal needed. When they get back into range, everything syncs. Works on iOS, Android, anything with a modern browser.

  • Photo equipment ID. Snap an HVAC plate, water heater label, or appliance nameplate. Chez Vision auto-extracts make, model, serial, and capacity. The system gets added to the customer's home profile automatically.
  • Pre-built visit checklist. Tasks from the dispatched visit + the homeowner's accumulated punch list + suggested first-visit setup prompts for new customers.
  • Offline drafts. Photos, notes, and checklist updates queue locally in IndexedDB. Connectivity pill shows online/offline. Drafts persist across phone reboots.
  • Live home profile. Tech sees every system on file, the open punch list, pending homeowner tasks, and recent invoices — context before they walk in.
  • Quick upsell capture. Spotted extra work? One tap creates a new homeowner request that lands on the dispatcher's queue back at HQ.

Team & roles

Scales from solo to a crew of 20.

Four roles, scoped permissions. Owners and admins manage the company. Dispatchers assign work. Technicians see only their own visits. Invite teammates via tokenized email links.

Owner

Full access. Set up the workspace, billing, integrations, and the directory listing. Invite admins, dispatchers, and technicians. Sign off the big quotes.

Administrator

Day-to-day operator. Manages crew, customers, quotes, and the line-item library. Right-hand to the owner. Doesn't see billing.

Dispatcher

Owns the queue. Assigns visits to techs with date, time window, and stop order. Sees the calendar, routes, and the dispatch board. Doesn't see crew billing.

Field technician

Mobile-first. Sees only their assigned visits. Runs the offline field app. Captures system photos, ticks the checklist, marks visits complete. No desk work.
