Your whole business in a single secure workspace.

Chez is built for the way handyman businesses actually run. Owners and admins manage the company. Dispatchers assign work. Technicians see only their own jobs. Invite your team via secure tokens. Everyone signs in to the same workspace with the right permissions for their role.

Dispatch & schedule

Assign visits, sequence routes, lock time windows.

Dispatchers assign each visit to a specific technician with a route date, time window, and stop order. Status-aware: visits only become assignable when they're ready. The calendar shows every upcoming assignment by tech. Customers see proposed dates and confirm — no phone tag.

  • Dispatch board. Drag visits onto technician slots with route date, time window, stop order.
  • Status-aware assignment. Visits only assignable when ready (submitted → scheduled → assignable).
  • Calendar view. All upcoming visits grouped by date and technician.
  • Visit coordination. Tech can confirm, propose alternate date, or decline before check-in. Eight-state machine drives the full handoff.
  • Two-way messaging. Provider ↔ homeowner thread per request. All scheduling clarifications live in one place.

Execute in the field

An offline-capable field app that does the paperwork while you do the work.

Open the visit on your phone or tablet — no internet required. Run the checklist your dispatcher built plus the homeowner's punch list. Snap a photo of any HVAC label, water heater plate, or appliance — Chez identifies it and adds it to the customer's record. Capture field notes. Mark complete. Sync drafts when you're back online.

  • Offline-ready field PWA. Works without connection. Installs to iOS/Android home screen via web app manifest.
  • Execution checklist. Tasks from your visit plan, the homeowner's punch list, and DIY items they want help with — all in one list.
  • Photo system capture. Snap any equipment label — Chez auto-identifies make, model, serial via the same Vision pipeline homeowners use.
  • First-visit setup prompts. Curated tasks for new customers ("set thermostat schedule," "confirm water shutoff location").
  • Quick-upsell capture. Spotted something else that needs work? Add it as a follow-up — becomes a new homeowner request automatically.
