Plan & schedule

Maintenance, organized by what's on schedule and what needs a decision.

The Maintenance Hub separates what's already happening (your services, your handyman, your vehicles) from what needs your attention (this season, upcoming visits). No mixing. No noise. Two yearly handyman visits keep your home's value high — automatically bundled from your accumulated punch list.

  • House Quiz. 37 questions across 6 sections. Save and resume. Ends with a cinematic reveal of the dollar value your maintenance plan protects over 10 years.
  • Maintenance Hub. Five sections: Your Services, Next Handyman Visit, Vehicles, This Season, Upcoming Scheduled.
  • Routine management. Recurring services (cleaning, lawn, snow, pool) as first-class objects. Active months. Pause windows. One vendor per routine.
  • Two yearly handyman visits. Spring and fall punch-list bundles auto-built from small DIY items you've accumulated all year.
  • Important events. Family calendar, school days, birthdays, recurring deliveries, trash days — with morning-of and evening-before reminders.

Estate & legacy

Estate intelligence, not estate storage.

Other apps store your will. Chez models it. The intake captures fiduciaries (linked to family members already in your app), advisors, wishes, and concerns. A readiness scorecard shows what's missing. When you're ready, generate a PDF for your attorney with a verifiable handoff link they can validate at getchez.com/verify.

  • Estate Intelligence intake. Six sections: household, advisors, concerns, fiduciaries, wishes, assets. Save per answer.
  • Readiness scorecard. 0–100 score. Staleness detection (3yr / 5yr / 7yr tiers). Auto-flagged when laws or family change.
  • Attorney handoff PDF. Verifiable token in the footer. 3-use limit. 7-day expiry. Public verification page.
  • Estate Snapshot. Read-only summary view to share with executors and trusted contacts.
  • Estate document detection. Forward a will or POA — Claude extracts execution dates, fiduciaries, and attorney info into your estate model.
