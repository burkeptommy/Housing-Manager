Estate & legacy

Estate intelligence, not estate storage.

Other apps store your will. Chez models it. The intake captures fiduciaries (linked to the family members already in your app), advisors, wishes, and concerns. A readiness scorecard shows what's missing. When you're ready, generate a PDF for your attorney with a verifiable handoff link they can validate at getchez.com/verify.

  • Estate Intelligence intake. Six sections: household, advisors, concerns, fiduciaries, wishes, assets. Save per answer.
  • Readiness scorecard. 0–100 score. Staleness detection (3yr / 5yr / 7yr tiers). Automatic flagging when laws or family change.
  • Attorney handoff PDF. Verifiable token in the footer. 3-use limit. 7-day expiry. Public verification page.
  • Estate Snapshot. Read-only summary view to share with executors and trusted contacts.
  • Estate document detection. Forward a will or POA — Claude extracts execution dates, fiduciaries, attorney info, and links them to your estate model.
