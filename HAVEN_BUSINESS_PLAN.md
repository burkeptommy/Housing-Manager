## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Haven is a full-service home management platform that combines human expertise with intelligent software to give homeowners their time back. Unlike household management apps that provide tools for homeowners to organize their own chaos, Haven provides dedicated Home Managers who actually handle the work—paying bills, coordinating vendors, scheduling maintenance, and managing the countless details that come with homeownership.

**Core Value Proposition:** "Stop managing your home. Start living in it."

**The Haven Difference:** One payment covers everything. One text handles anything. From paying bills to fixing leaks, Haven provides results—not more to-do lists.

---

## THE PROBLEM

### Homeownership Has Become a Second Job

Modern homeowners juggle careers, families, and personal lives while also serving as the unpaid project manager of their own home:

- **12+ vendor relationships** to manage (plumbers, electricians, landscapers, HVAC, etc.)
- **Dozens of bills** arriving at random times from different providers
- **Hours spent on hold** with contractors and service providers
- **Forgotten maintenance** leading to expensive emergency repairs
- **Constant mental overhead** remembering what needs to be done when

**The average homeowner spends 8+ hours per month** on home management tasks—time that could be spent with family, pursuing hobbies, or simply relaxing.

### Current Solutions Fall Short

**Household Management Software ($375+/month + $3-5K setup):**
- Provides tools but homeowner still does all the work
- Digital contact lists don't make phone calls
- Tracking maintenance doesn't fix the furnace
- Software can't negotiate with vendors or oversee contractors

**Traditional Estate Management (Luxury Only):**
- Services costing $5,000-$15,000+/month
- Only accessible to ultra-high-net-worth individuals
- Overkill for typical homeowners

**The Gap:** Professional home management shouldn't require a trust fund.

---

## THE SOLUTION

### Haven: Professional Home Management for Everyone

Haven bridges the gap between expensive estate management and DIY chaos by providing:

1. **Dedicated Home Manager**
   - Real person who knows your home and preferences
   - Single point of contact for all home-related needs
   - Proactive management, not reactive firefighting
   
2. **Bill Consolidation**
   - One monthly payment covers ALL household bills
   - Haven pays mortgage, utilities, insurance, vendors, subscriptions
   - Zero markup on pass-through costs
   - Automatic, on-time payments every time

3. **Vendor Coordination**
   - Haven finds, vets, and manages qualified contractors
   - All scheduling and oversight handled by your manager
   - Negotiation on homeowner's behalf
   - Quality assurance on completed work

4. **Preventive Maintenance**
   - Monthly handyman visits catch issues early
   - Scheduled service for all home systems
   - Small problems fixed before becoming emergencies
   - Comprehensive maintenance tracking

5. **Life Management (Haven+)**
   - Errand running and pickups
   - Package handling and returns
   - Travel coordination
   - Event planning assistance

## KEY ROLES & OPERATIONS

### The Haven Team Structure

**Home Manager (Sarah)**
- Manages 15-25 households
- Single point of contact for homeowners
- Coordinates all vendors and services
- Handles approvals, scheduling, oversight
- Builds relationships with households

**Handyman (Mike/Carlos/Maria)**
- Dedicated to geographic region
- Monthly preventive visits
- Minor repairs and maintenance
- Eyes and ears in the home
- Reports issues before they escalate

**Operations Support**
- Bill processing and payments
- Vendor onboarding and vetting
- Customer success
- Platform operations

### Technology Platform

The Haven app serves as the central hub:

- **Dashboard:** Daily overview, manager messages, action items
- **Sarah (Manager Hub):** Direct access to your Home Manager
- **Messages:** Unified inbox for all home communications
- **Calendar:** Family and home schedule integration
- **Your Home:** Property details, systems, documents
- **Family:** Household members, logistics, lifestyle data
- **Projects:** Home improvement tracking
- **Maintenance:** Systems health and service scheduling
- **Find Pros:** Vetted contractor directory
- **Money:** Bills, statements, spending overview
- **Tasks:** Household and manager operations
- **Inventory:** Shopping lists and procurement
